Screen Limit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Screen Limit is an Android app that helps you limit the time you spend in the apps you choose. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and we do not collect, store or share your data.

What the app uses

  • App usage information. Through Android's "Usage access" permission, the app can see which app is in the foreground and for how long. It uses this to work out how much time you have spent today in the apps you chose, to enforce your limits, and to show your history.
  • The list of apps on your phone. The app reads the names and icons of your installed apps so you can pick which ones to limit.
  • Your settings. The apps you chose, your daily limits, your lock modes, and daily usage totals for those apps (so history can last longer than Android keeps it).
  • Partner codes. If you create partner codes, only a one-way scrambled version (a hash) is stored on your phone. The codes themselves are shown once and are not kept in readable form.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Usage access: to measure time spent in the apps you limited.
  • Display over other apps: to show the "Time's up" screen on top of an app whose limit has been reached.
  • Notifications: to show that protection is on, and to warn you if it stops.
  • Run at startup and foreground service: to keep protection running after a restart and while the screen is on.

Screen Limit does not use Android's Accessibility service, does not read what is on your screen, and does not read your messages, contacts, photos or location.

Data collection and sharing

Screen Limit does not have an internet permission. It cannot send information off your phone. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not receive any.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays on your phone until you remove an app from your limits, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Screen Limit. Uninstalling removes all of it. App data is excluded from Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer.

Children

Screen Limit is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.
